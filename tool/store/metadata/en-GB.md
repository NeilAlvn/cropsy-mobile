## description
Grow your own vegetables and herbs, anywhere: a windowsill, a balcony, a raised bed or an allotment. Cropsy tells you which crops fit your space and exactly when to sow, plant out, water and harvest them.

YOUR SEASON, LAID OUT
Pick a crop and press Start. Cropsy builds a season path from your own frost dates: sow indoors, plant out, first harvest, last harvest. When the weather shifts, the plan moves with it. You are never behind.

ONE PLACE FOR THE WHOLE GARDEN
Home shows what needs doing this week. My garden holds every plant with its photos and notes. Season path shows the year at a glance. Explore finds the next crop for the space you have.

REMINDERS THAT KNOW YOUR POTS
Watering reminders follow the size of the container and the forecast, so a small pot in a heatwave and a bed in April get different advice.

DIAGNOSE FROM A PHOTO
Spots on a leaf? Take a picture. Cropsy suggests the likely problem, what to do today and how to prevent it next time. Every answer says it is a guess, because it is.

A JOURNAL THAT ADDS UP
Log every harvest in grams or pieces and watch the yearly tally grow. Growth photos stay with the plant.
